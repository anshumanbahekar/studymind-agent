How adaptive difficulty works
StudyMind tracks your accuracy on easy, medium, and hard cards in real time. If you're acing easy cards, harder questions get mixed in automatically. If you're struggling, the system backs off to rebuild confidence — this is your Zone of Proximal Development, recalculated after every answer.
